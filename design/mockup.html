How this differs from the app that already ships

Nothing here is a reskin of an existing Blinkit feature under a new name. Each row below names the real surface a user has today, what changes, and which piece of research justifies the change.

Dimension Already in production New: Occasion Concierge Why (research)
Entry point Generic homepage: same banner and category grid for every user, every session. Screen 02A single opt-in prompt card. Nothing is injected into the home layout — the user chooses to open it. "The homepage feels repetitive. I see the same products every time." — Interview 3
How suggestions are chosen Algorithmic cross-sell / "you may also like," inferred from purchase history, not from a stated need. Screen 03Generated from what the user says, refined by one clarifying question — relevance is explicit, not inferred. "Most recommendations aren't related to my shopping list." — Interview 2
Where trust signals live Ratings exist, but only inside a product's own page — a user must already be considering that exact item to see them. Screen 04Rating + review snippet + "why this" shown inline, at the moment the item is suggested — no extra tap required. Reviews and nutritional detail were what turned uncertainty into a purchase. — Interview 4; AI insight (moderate strength)
When discovery happens The one discovery moment on record today is at checkout, via a discount that happens to appear. Discovery happens when the need is stated, before the cart is built — shaping the order, not tailing it. "Only when something appeared during checkout with a discount." — Interview 1
Reorder-mode sessions No separation — the same homepage serves a 3-minute reorder and a browsing session identically. Concierge is a separate, user-initiated screen (03/04). Buy Again, search and categories are untouched — zero added friction for speed-only sessions. Part 1 discovery engine flagged segment/mode differences as unanswerable from review data alone — this is the gap primary research filled.
On the review snippets in Screen 04: they're written in the register of real reviews already validated in the Part 1 corpus (discovery_engine/data/processed/corpus.jsonl) — short, specific, unpolished. In the shipped agent, these become literal retrieved quotes via the same embedding index `chat_app.py` already uses, not paraphrases invented at design time.
