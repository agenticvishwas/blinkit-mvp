How this differs from the app that already ships

Nothing here is a reskin of an existing Blinkit feature under a new name. Each row below names the real surface a user has today, what changes, and which piece of research justifies the change. The production app already does more occasion merchandising than the first design pass assumed — the table below was corrected against a live screenshot audit, not left as an untested guess.

Dimension Already in production New: Occasion Concierge Why (research)
Occasion merchandising Already extensive — Gifting Corner, Party With Friends, Date Night Edit, a seasonal IP store, all live on the home screen today. This is not a gap. Screen 03Responds to any occasion the user types, not just the ones merchandising picked this week — and personalizes to specifics (headcount, preference) a static tile can't ask for. Confirmed by live screenshot audit, 2026-08-01 — this row was corrected after seeing the real app; the earlier draft understated how much occasion merchandising already exists.
How suggestions are chosen A merchandising team pre-picks a fixed set of tiles, same for every user, on a campaign calendar. Screen 03Generated from what the user says, refined by one clarifying question — relevance is explicit, not merchandising-curated. "Most recommendations aren't related to my shopping list." — Interview 2
Where trust signals live Zero — none of the merchandising tiles (Gifting Corner, Party With Friends, etc.) show a rating or review anywhere on the tile or its landing page preview. Screen 04Rating + review snippet + "why this" shown inline, at the moment the item is suggested — no extra tap required. Reviews and nutritional detail were what turned uncertainty into a purchase. — Interview 4; AI insight (moderate strength)
When discovery happens Either passively on the home screen (tiles) or reactively at checkout (discount upsell) — never elicited from a stated need. Discovery happens when the need is stated, before the cart is built — shaping the order, not tailing it. "Only when something appeared during checkout with a discount." — Interview 1
Reorder-mode sessions No separation — the same home screen with the same merchandising tiles serves a 3-minute reorder and a browsing session identically. Concierge is a separate, user-initiated screen (03/04). Buy Again, search, categories and every merchandising tile are untouched — zero added friction for speed-only sessions. Part 1 discovery engine flagged segment/mode differences as unanswerable from review data alone — this is the gap primary research filled.
On the review snippets in Screen 04: they're written in the register of real reviews already validated in the Part 1 corpus (discovery_engine/data/processed/corpus.jsonl) — short, specific, unpolished. In the shipped agent, these become literal retrieved quotes via the same embedding index `chat_app.py` already uses, not paraphrases invented at design time.
